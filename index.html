Safety rules for the day

  • Gloves on at all times: Wear your provided gloves for the entire duration of the cleanup. Never handle trash with bare hands.
  • Appropriate footwear: Closed-toe shoes (sturdy sneakers or boots) are strictly required. No sandals, flip-flops, or bare feet.
  • Sun & weather protection: Apply sunscreen, wear a hat, and dress in layers appropriate for the coastal weather.
  • Leave nature alone: Leave seaweed, seagrass, driftwood, shells, stones, and all local wildlife undisturbed.
  • No entering the water: Nobody is allowed to enter the water – not even to retrieve a floating item or debris.
  • Zero-touch hazards: Never touch sharps, syringes, chemical containers, unknown substances, gas cylinders, flares, or anything that looks like ammunition. Action: Mark the spot, keep others away, and immediately notify the safety lead.
  • Team lifting: For heavy or awkward debris, lift together in pairs. If it is too heavy or unsafe, leave it in place and report its location to the team lead.
  • Family policy: Family members stay on the sand/shoreline at all times; minors must be directly supervised by their parent or guardian.
  • Stay hydrated: Take regular water breaks, especially on warm days.
  • First Aid & Injuries: Report any cuts, scrapes, stings, or feeling unwell immediately to the safety lead or first-aid responder – even minor scratches need disinfecting.
